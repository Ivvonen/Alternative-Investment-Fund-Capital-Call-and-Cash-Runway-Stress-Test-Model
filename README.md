## Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) Capital Call and Cash Runway Stress Test Model

This risk model is designed to simulate the capital call dynamics, funding liquidity limits, and capital cushion degradation of Private Equity, Private Debt, and Real Estate funds. This interactive model simulates a macro-prudential liquidity freeze under severe **Alternative Investment Fund Managers Directive (AIFMD)** stress scenarios. 

The model maps the non-linear cash flow mismatch known as the "Unfunded Commitment Problem" and tracks the direct impact of investor defaults on a fund’s operational survival horizon.
